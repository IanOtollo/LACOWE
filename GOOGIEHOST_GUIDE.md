# GoogieHost Guide - 100% Truly Free (No Sales Pressure)

GoogieHost is one of the best alternatives for a "quiet" experience. They use LiteSpeed servers, which are often faster than InfinityFree.

## Step 1: Sign Up
1. Visit [GoogieHost.com](https://googiehost.com/free-hosting.html).
2. Click **"Get Free Hosting"**.
3. Choose a free subdomain (e.g., `lacowe.onlinewebshop.net` or `lacowe.66ghz.com`).
4. Complete the signup. **No credit card required.**

## Step 2: Access DirectAdmin
Unlike Hostinger's sales-heavy panel, GoogieHost usually uses **DirectAdmin** or a custom panel that is focused only on your files.
1. Check your email for login details.
2. Log in to your control panel.

## Step 3: Database & Files
1. Look for **"MySQL Management"**.
2. Create your database, user, and password.
3. Open the **"File Manager"** and go to `public_html`.
4. Upload all your files there.

## Step 4: Update Config
Edit `config/config.php` on the server:
- `DB_HOST`: Often `localhost` or a specific IP provided in the panel.
- `DB_NAME`, `DB_USER`, `DB_PASS`: As created in Step 3.

## Why GoogleHost?
- **SSD Storage**: Much faster than the spinning disks on many free hosts.
- **LiteSpeed**: Faster page loads for PHP apps.
- **No Ads**: They don't put ads on your site or spam your panel with $9.99/mo deals.
